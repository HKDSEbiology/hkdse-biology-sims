# HKDSE Biology Interactive Simulations

**Free classroom simulations for Hong Kong DSE Biology** — coordination (cerebral cortex) and genetics (genotype → phenotype).

Created & developed by **Ms Lai Kam Fung** for secondary Biology / Combined Science teaching.

🌐 **Open the hub:** [GitHub Pages cover page](https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/hkdse-biology-sims/)  
*(Replace `YOUR_USERNAME` after you enable Pages.)*

## Simulations

| Simulation | Topic | What students do |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| [**Probe the Sensory Cortex**](probe-the-sensory-cortex.html) | Human coordination · cerebrum · sensory area | Mosquito tip on the body → opposite sensory cortex lights up; pain sensation |
| [**Probe the Motor Cortex**](probe-the-brain.html) | Human coordination · cerebrum · motor area | Probe the motor area → opposite body side moves (**cross-control**) |
| [**Make a Baby**](make-a-baby-app/chromosome-sim.html) | Genetics · inheritance · variation | Select gamete chromosomes, fertilise, see phenotype + Activity 1 worksheet |

Start from the [**cover page**](index.html) so students can find every simulation in one place.
